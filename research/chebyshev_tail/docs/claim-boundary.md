# Claim boundary

| Statement | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `psi(x)` is the sum of `log p` over prime powers `p^k <= x` | Definition | finite implementation follows the definition |
| The displayed tail values equal `psi(x)-x` on the selected finite grid | Computed fact | reproducible from the versioned script |
| A non-real off-critical zero has a fourfold symmetry orbit | Published theorem | conjugation and the functional equation give the orbit |
| A finite sample proves that no off-critical zero exists | Rejected | finite samples cannot establish a universal statement |
| Five primes give a controlled Euler-product approximation in the critical strip | Rejected | the Euler product is absolutely convergent only for `Re(s)>1` |
| A large tail diagnostic identifies a zero | Hypothesis generator only | cancellation, truncation, and numerical error must be excluded first |
| The scripts prove RH | Rejected | no finite computation supplies the required global implication |

The module uses two categories only: exact finite identities and finite numerical
experiments.  The absence of a signal is not evidence of absence of a zero.
