\begin{abstract}
We do \textbf{not} prove Full BSD for All Elliptic Curves.
We formulate an open hypothesis and provide partial numerical evidence.
The conjecture is stated as an \texttt{axiom} with a \texttt{TODO}
marker in all formal proof assistants.
\end{abstract}

\section{Open Hypothesis (BSD\_C)}
\begin{quote}
  \textbf{Hypothesis BSD\_C (open).}~rank equals order of vanishing for all elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$
\end{quote}

\section{Status}
This statement is \emph{not proven}.  It is recorded as
\verb|axiom BSD\_C ... -- TODO PROVAR| in Lean~4, Coq, and Isabelle/HOL.
No claim is made regarding the Millennium Prize.

\section{Evidence}
Numerical simulation provides circumstantial evidence only.
A rigorous proof would require techniques beyond current methods.
