\begin{abstract}
We present a software artefact that separates assumptions, computable
definitions, proof obligations and mechanically checked finite theorems. It
uses strong Kleene three-valued logic as an epistemic interface and a complex
action as an illustrative numerical model. It makes no claim to solve an open
Millennium Prize Problem.
\end{abstract}
\section{Status discipline}
An axiom extends a theory; it is not a theorem of the unextended theory. The
value $U$ records an undecided proposition, not a dialetheic conjunction.
\section{Illustrative action}
\[S[\gamma]=\int_0^T L(\gamma,\dot\gamma)\,dt+i\int_0^T e(t)\,dt.\]
Numerical integration supplies evidence about a chosen path only.

\section{Formal artefacts}
Lean, Coq, Isabelle/HOL and Agda modules prove the finite K3 truth-table laws.
Open mathematical statements remain explicitly assumed or uninhabited.
\section{Reproducibility and limitations}
All plots are generated by a versioned script. Compiler verification must be
reported with exact toolchain versions. Visualizations, network traces and
cryptographic experiments are not proofs of open mathematical statements.
